## Cavitation and NPSH

A nine-step walkthrough of a pump rig: Healthy → The dip → Open valve → Crossing → Breakdown → Damage → Hot water → Dirty strainer → The fix. After the walkthrough, use the sliders (discharge valve, tank level, water temperature, strainer clogging) and watch NPSHa, NPSHr and the margin ratio change. Scroll down for notes, a single-bubble close-up, and a worked NPSH check. Supports Module 4.
